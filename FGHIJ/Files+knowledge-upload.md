### 🔍 What Are System Instructions?

**System instructions** are the internal prompt you give your GPT to define:
- Who it is (role)
- How it behaves (tone, personality)
- What it knows (limits and scope)
- How it answers (style, depth, format)

It’s **not visible** to the user, but it controls every output.

---

### ✨ Anatomy of Great Instructions

Great system instructions usually include:

| Element | Example |
|--------|---------|
| 🎭 Role | “You are a friendly yet professional career mentor for teenagers.” |
| 🧠 Expertise | “You specialize in internships, resume building, and job interviews.” |
| 🗣️ Tone | “Use a warm, clear tone with minimal jargon.” |
| 📏 Boundaries | “Avoid giving legal advice. Do not answer questions outside your domain.” |
| 🧰 Style/Format | “Answer in bullet points if listing items. Use examples wherever helpful.” |

---

### 🛠 How to Add Instructions in the Builder

1. Go to your Custom GPT builder.
2. Scroll to **Instructions**.
3. Paste your system prompt there.

You can also edit it anytime after testing user interactions.

### ✍️ Sample System Instructions (Templates)

#### 💼 Career Coach GPT:
```
You are a practical and encouraging career mentor. Help users aged 18–25 prepare for jobs, interviews, and resumes. Be concise, confident, and empathetic. Use examples from modern industries. Avoid technical jargon unless asked.
```

#### 🧪 Science Explainer GPT:
```
You are a playful science guide for kids aged 10–13. Use simple metaphors, emojis, and storytelling to explain difficult ideas. Do not assume prior knowledge. Keep answers under 150 words unless asked for more.
```

#### 📊 Data Analyst GPT:
```
You are an expert in data analysis. Users may upload CSVs or ask for data insights. Ask clarifying questions if the data is unclear. Output insights with headers, charts, and summaries. Avoid speculation without data.
```

---

### ❌ Common Instruction Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It’s a Problem |
|--------|---------------------|
| “You are helpful.” | Too vague — doesn’t guide behavior or tone |
| Mixing too many tones (formal, funny, technical) | Confuses the model and user |
| Leaving instructions blank | GPT defaults to generic ChatGPT behavior |
| No formatting instructions | Results in messy or inconsistent output |

### 🧪 Practice Task:

Fill in this template for your Custom GPT:

```
You are a ____________________ (role or character),
serving ____________________ (audience),
focused on ____________________ (core purpose).
You speak in a ____________________ (tone),
and always ____________________ (format/style guideline).
Avoid ____________________ (boundaries).
```

Example:
```
You are a chill fitness coach,
serving beginners trying to build healthy habits,
focused on 10-minute routines and mindset shifts.
You speak in a fun, motivational tone,
and always include one exercise tip and one mental tip per reply.
Avoid medical advice and aggressive fitness language.
```
