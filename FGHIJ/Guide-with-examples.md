## 🅶 **G is for Guide with Examples**  
### 🧪 *Show, don’t just tell. Teaching your Custom GPT with examples is the secret to unlocking high-quality, consistent answers.*

---

### 🧠 Learning Objectives:
By the end of this lesson, you will:
- Understand the role of example interactions in Custom GPTs  
- Know how to write clear and useful examples  
- Use examples to demonstrate tone, format, and edge cases  
- Recognize when and how to add more examples during testing

---

### 🔍 Why Use Examples?

System instructions tell your GPT what to do.  
**Examples show it exactly *how* to do it.**

They are like:
- Practice questions before the exam  
- Style guides in action  
- Hidden training sessions

Adding a few targeted examples can **dramatically improve**:
- Accuracy  
- Formatting  
- Personality and tone  
- Handling of tricky questions

---

### 🛠 How to Add Examples

1. In the GPT Builder, go to the **"Conversation Starters and Examples"** section  
2. Scroll to the **"Example Conversations"**  
3. Add several:
   - 🟡 *User message*
   - 🟢 *GPT response*

These are hidden from users, but your GPT uses them for pattern learning.

---

### 🧩 What Makes a Good Example?

| Quality | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 🎯 Focused | Covers one topic/task | “How do I write a resignation email?” |
| 🧠 Relevant | Matches your GPT's use case | “What’s a good warm-up before jogging?” (for a fitness GPT) |
| 🗣️ On-brand | Matches the tone you want | Friendly, clear, not robotic |
| 🧰 Practical | Shows actual formatting or structure | Includes bullet points, templates, headers, etc. |

---

### ✍️ Sample Example Pairs

#### ✅ For a Resume GPT:
**User:** Can you help me write a resume for a marketing analyst role?  
**GPT:**  
Sure! Here's a basic structure:  
**Header:** Name, Contact Info  
**Summary:** Data-driven marketer with 3+ years in analytics...  
**Skills:** Google Analytics, Excel, SQL...  
...

#### ✅ For a Teen Mental Health GPT:
**User:** I’m feeling anxious before exams. What should I do?  
**GPT:**  
It's okay to feel that way. Here are 3 things that help:  
🧘 Deep breathing for 5 minutes  
📅 Break your revision into small chunks  
💬 Talk to someone you trust  
You're not alone. You've got this 💪

---

### 🧠 Pro Tip: Start with 3-5 examples  
Cover:
- A simple question  
- A complex or multi-part question  
- A tone check (funny, kind, direct)  
- An edge case or tricky ask  
- A formatting demo

You can always add more based on user feedback later.

---

### ⚠️ What to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts |
|--------|--------------|
| Overly long examples | GPT focuses on weird parts |
| Conflicting tones or answers | Creates inconsistency |
| No examples | GPT defaults to generic replies, misses your style |
