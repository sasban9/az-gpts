## 🅴 **E is for Extend with Knowledge**
### 📚 *Upgrade your GPT from generalist to expert by uploading files and giving it custom knowledge.*

---

### 🧠 Learning Objectives:
By the end of this lesson, you will:
- Learn how to upload files and extend GPT’s knowledge
- Understand what kinds of files work best
- Discover creative ways to make your GPT smarter with minimal input
- Avoid overloading your GPT with unfocused data

---

### 🔍 Why Extend Knowledge?

Custom GPTs are powerful, but their native knowledge cuts off at 2023 (or may not cover your exact needs).

By uploading your own data, you can:
- Train a GPT on **internal documents**
- Share **company policies or brand guides**
- Give access to **class notes, books, manuals, spreadsheets, etc.**
- Build a GPT that **answers only from your own content**

---

### 📁 Section 1: What You Can Upload

Supported formats:
- ✅ PDFs (e.g. training manuals, books)
- ✅ DOCX or TXT (notes, transcripts, essays)
- ✅ CSV (tables, lists, records)
- ✅ Code files (for technical GPTs)

Max total size: **20 files, 100MB total**

---

### 🛠️ Section 2: How to Add Knowledge in the GPT Builder

1. Open your Custom GPT in the builder
2. Scroll to **“Knowledge”**
3. Click **Upload files**
4. Add your PDFs, docs, spreadsheets, or more

Optional:
- Use the "Instructions" box to guide the GPT:  
  > “Only answer questions using the uploaded employee handbook.”  
  > “Do not hallucinate beyond the content provided.”

---

### 🧪 Pro Tip: Name your files clearly  
Instead of `file1.pdf`, try:  
- `Company_Brand_Guide_2024.pdf`  
- `Customer_FAQ_April2025.txt`  
- `Sabya_Science_Notes_Grade4.pdf`

---

### 🧠 Section 3: Smart Strategies for Custom Knowledge

| Strategy | Example Use |
|----------|-------------|
| 🧑‍🎓 Class Companion | Upload course notes + syllabus → GPT tutor |
| 🏢 Company Bot | Upload SOPs + manuals → onboarding assistant |
| 🧠 Niche Expert | Upload your ebook → GPT that answers only from it |
| 📊 Analyst Assistant | Upload CSVs → GPT that analyzes data or trends |
| 🖼️ Portfolio Bot | Upload case studies → GPT that presents your work |

---

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It’s a Problem |
|--------|---------------------|
| Uploading messy or scanned PDFs | GPT may misread or skip poorly formatted data |
| No clear instructions in system prompt | GPT might hallucinate or ignore your content |
| Too many unrelated files | Confuses the GPT, dilutes quality |
