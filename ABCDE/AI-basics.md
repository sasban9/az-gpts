### 📚 Section 1: What is GPT?

**GPT stands for**:
- **G**enerative – It can create (generate) new text based on prompts
- **P**retrained – It’s trained on a vast dataset before you use it
- **T**ransformer – A neural network architecture designed to handle sequential data like language

GPTs are like supercharged text prediction machines. You give it a prompt, it continues the thought — like autocomplete, but way smarter.

---

### ⚙️ Section 2: How GPT Works (Simple Terms)

- GPT reads your input, **tokenizes** it (breaks it into chunks)
- It predicts the most likely next word based on patterns it has learned
- The output depends heavily on **prompt structure** and **context window**
- It doesn’t “think” — it predicts the next best word using probabilities

> 🧩 *Think of GPT like a mind trained on the internet, guessing what should come next based on what it’s read billions of times before.*

---

### 🆚 Section 3: GPT-3.5 vs GPT-4 vs Custom GPTs

| Model | Speed | Cost | Intelligence | Use Case |
|-------|-------|------|--------------|----------|
| GPT-3.5 | Fast | Lower | Good general use | Lightweight tasks |
| GPT-4 | Slower | Higher | More nuanced, deeper reasoning | Critical/complex tasks |
| **Custom GPT** | Based on GPT-4-turbo | Personalized | Task-specific, instruction-locked | Use for assistants, support bots, educators |

> ✅ **Custom GPTs = GPT-4 turbo + Your personality + Tools + Optional files**

---

### 🌐 Section 4: Where Do Custom GPTs Fit?

Custom GPTs allow **non-coders** to create personalized AI agents with:
- A defined **persona**
- Clear **instructions**
- Optional **tools** (e.g. browsing, file uploads, code)
- Knowledge of **your content** (uploaded files)

They are perfect for:
- Solo entrepreneurs
- Educators
- Business teams
- Researchers
- Coaches
- Customer service automation
