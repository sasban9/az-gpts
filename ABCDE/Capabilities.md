---

### 🔧 Section 1: What Are “Capabilities”?

Capabilities are **special powers** you can give your Custom GPT. They extend the GPT’s functions beyond just text.

Here are the **three core capabilities**:

| Capability | What It Does | Use It For |
|------------|--------------|------------|
| 🌍 **Browsing** | Lets GPT search the web in real-time | Up-to-date information, news, research |
| 📊 **Code Interpreter (a.k.a. Python/Advanced Data Analysis)** | Runs Python code and analyzes files | Math, graphs, file parsing, simulations |
| 🎨 **DALL·E Image Generator** | Creates images from prompts | Visual storytelling, marketing, education |

---

### 🚀 Section 2: When Should You Use Each?

#### 🌍 **Browsing**
✅ Enable when your GPT needs:
- Real-time stock prices, weather, or news  
- To look up external references or URLs  
- To help users discover online tools or articles

🔒 Don’t enable if:
- Your GPT is knowledge-locked (e.g. based on internal PDF content)

---

#### 📊 **Code Interpreter (a.k.a. Python)**
✅ Enable for:
- Data analysis (CSV, Excel, JSON)
- Budgeting tools or calculators
- Generating charts or simulations
- File conversion and inspection

🔒 Not needed if:
- Your GPT is strictly conversational or instructional

---

#### 🎨 **DALL·E (Image Generator)**
✅ Enable for:
- Logo generation  
- Teaching visual concepts  
- Creative projects (cartooning, storyboarding)  
- Personalized greeting cards or memes  

🔒 Avoid if:
- Your GPT doesn’t rely on visual creativity

---

### 🛠️ Section 3: How to Enable Capabilities in the Builder

1. While building your GPT, scroll to the **"Add Capabilities"** section.
2. Toggle on:
   - ✅ Browsing
   - ✅ Code Interpreter
   - ✅ DALL·E
3. Save or publish your GPT

You can enable all three, or just the ones you need.
